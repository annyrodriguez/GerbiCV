Mentorship & Student Research

Empowering the Next Generation of Public Health Leaders

As Director of Public Health Research at Morehouse School of Medicine (MSM),
Dr. Gemechu B. Gerbi has made mentorship a cornerstone of his academic career.
He works closely with students to design rigorous research questions, apply
epidemiologic and biostatistical methods, interpret national surveillance data,
and present actionable findings to improve population health.

His work prioritizes health disparities and equity, ensuring students confront
real-world public health challenges and contribute to solutions that matter.

Highlights of Mentorship Impact

– 100+ student-led research presentations at APHA, AACR, RCMI, MSM Research Symposiums, and national health disparities conferences.

– Mentorship producing peer-reviewed manuscripts and first-author student publications.

– Leadership of MPH research initiatives advancing careers in: - Public health analytics - Applied epidemiology - Cancer prevention - Chronic disease equity - Behavioral and mental health research

“Dr. Gerbi supports students in transforming data into impact.”

Student Research Focus Areas

  • Cancer screening among underrepresented populations
  • Chronic disease burden (heart disease, COPD, stroke)
  • Adolescent mental health and violence exposure
  • Sexual and reproductive health (HPV, PID, HIV)
  • Health-related behaviors & quality of life
  • Youth risk behaviors using YRBSS
  • Maternal & child health disparities

All rooted in national datasets, including BRFSS, NSFG, and NHANES.

Mentorship Philosophy

“Public health research is not only about data — it is about shaping leaders
who know how to use evidence to transform communities.”

Dr. Gerbi cultivates: - Independence in analytical decision-making
- Clarity in scientific communication
- Confidence in professional presentation
- Commitment to community impact
